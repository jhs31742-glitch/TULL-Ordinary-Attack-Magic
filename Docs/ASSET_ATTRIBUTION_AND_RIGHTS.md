## Important permission boundaries
Credit is not a replacement for permission.
The Flying Animations - Resource page has credited asset-use permission,
but warns that other authors' assets may need separate permission.
Modification and Donation Points have further conditions.
Derived firearm animation redistribution is not automatically cleared.
Do not relicense external HKX, original game assets or imported works
under the MIT or CC BY licenses of the original TOAM materials.
